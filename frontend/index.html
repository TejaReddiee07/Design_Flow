          FAQ

          Questions, answered.

          Can't find what you need? Ask us directly and we reply within one business day.

          How does payment work?
          Each request has a fixed price shown before you order. You create a free account, submit the brief, and pay securely for that request. No subscription, no contract.
          How fast will I get my designs?
          Every service lists its delivery time, from 1 day for thumbnails and banners to 5 days for a pitch deck or company profile. You can track progress live in your portal.
          What do the AI agents do, and what does a human do?
          Agents handle research, copy, concept directions, quality checks and file delivery. A designer reviews every concept, finishes the visuals and approves what goes out. Nothing is delivered without approval.
          Can I request revisions?
          Yes, unlimited on every order. Leave feedback on a delivery and it goes straight back into the pipeline with priority.
          Which currencies can I pay in?
          US dollars, British pounds or Indian rupees. Choose your currency when you create your account; every price is shown in it.
          Who owns the work?
          You do. All final files, source files and rights are yours once delivered.
          What file formats do I get?
          Whatever the job needs: PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF, print-ready PDFs with bleed, plus editable sources (Figma, AI, PSD, PPTX or Google Slides).
