Common questions

How does payment work?
Each request has a fixed price ($13, £10 or ₹1090 starting price, quoted per request). You create an account, submit the brief, pay securely, and we start. No subscription, no contract.
How many revisions can I ask for?
Unlimited. Leave feedback on the delivery and the request re-enters the pipeline with priority.
What if I don't have brand guidelines yet?
Share whatever you have: a logo, a few colours, sites you admire. The Strategy Agent fills the gaps and your designer proposes a direction. Many clients start with a logo and build from there.
